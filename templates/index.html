Frequently asked questions

Are all conversions perfect?

No. PDF layout and tables can vary. PDF-to-PPTX creates slide images; it does not make the slide text editable. Scanned PDFs may require OCR.

How long are download links available?

By default, files expire after two hours. The host may also remove temporary files when a service restarts.
